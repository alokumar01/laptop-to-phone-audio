## Golden Rule
For every problem I solve in April, I must classify it into:

- `A` = solved cleanly without help
- `B` = solved with hint / partial struggle
- `C` = failed / forgot pattern / too slow

Priority of revision:
- first revise `C`
- then revise `B`
- then revise `A`

If I do not track this, revision becomes fake.

---

## Daily Revision Structure
Whenever I sit for DSA revision, I should not ask "what topic now?"
I should follow this structure.

### Block 1: Warm-up
- 1 easy or medium from yesterday's topic
- time limit: 20 minutes

### Block 2: Main Pattern Practice
- 2 problems from today's topic
- 1 should be standard pattern
- 1 should be slightly tricky

### Block 3: Old Weak Problem
- 1 problem from `B` or `C` list

### Block 4: Timed Problem
- 1 timed medium or hard
- time limit:
  - medium: 30 minutes
  - hard: 45 minutes

### Block 5: Analysis
After solving, note:
- pattern
- mistake
- why I got stuck
- whether it is `A`, `B`, or `C`

Daily total:
- 4 to 5 serious problems

On contest days:
- contest itself counts as problem practice
